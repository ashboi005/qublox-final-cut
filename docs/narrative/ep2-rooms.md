# Episode 2: The Set (Abandoned Soundstages) — Room-by-Room Beats

> **Director's Shot List**  
> **Setting:** Historic Film Soundstages overrun by Green-Screen Voids & Stock Families  
> **Target Length:** 7 Rooms (Vector Bridges & Color Swap Puzzles)

---

### Room 01: Soundstage 01 — The Green Screen Void
- **Scene:** A massive hollow soundstage where physical practical sets have been stripped away and coated in sickly flat lime-green chroma-key paint.
- **The Gag:** A crew of Stock Photo Interns in matching polo shirts are pantomiming having fun in front of empty air, shouting scripted buzzwords.
- **Crew Focus:** Movement & combat basics with Tushar and Sehaj; clearing stock actor drones.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Billboard: `"PRODUCE CONTENT. SMILE FOREVER."`
  - Green-screen cyclorama wall with missing tracking markers.
  - Tushar muttering: `"Practical effects are dead. I weep for celluloid."`

---

### Room 02: The Prop Warehouse — The Vector Chasm
- **Scene:** Deep cavernous warehouse where historic movie props (giant rubber boulders, cardboard spaceships) hang from frayed ropes over a spike pit.
- **The Gag:** Mansukh is furiously yelling at an automated client drone that is trying to stretch a cinema poster non-uniformly.
- **Crew Focus:** **Mansukh's PEN TOOL**. Click two anchor points to draw a glowing vector line platform spanning the gap. Mansukh joins party!
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Neon sign: `"ASPECT RATIO 16:9 — DO NOT DISTORT"`
  - Crates labeled: `"PRACTICAL SPECIAL EFFECTS (OBSOLETE)"`
  - Vector bezier handles floating in mid-air.

---

### Room 03: The Lighting Grid — Chromatic Swaps
- **Scene:** High-altitude catwalks suspended under theatrical lighting trusses. Color-coded force-field blocks block the pathway.
- **The Gag:** Manya is hurling paint buckets at a beige wall while screaming color hex codes like battle cries.
- **Crew Focus:** **Manya's FILL BUCKET**. Painting blocks changes their physical state: Red becomes solid, Blue becomes passable, Yellow becomes bouncy! Manya joins party!
- **Props & Signage:**
  - CRT screen: `"Red = Solid | Blue = Walk-Through | Yellow = Bouncy"`
  - Stencil: `"SATURATION IS FREEDOM"`
  - Paint splatter on warning signs.

---

### Room 04: The Chroma Key Dressing Rooms
- **Scene:** Labyrinth of dressing room corridors where stock photo actors spawn continuously from illuminated makeup vanity mirrors.
- **The Gag:** Mirrors give characters corporate headshot hairstyles and frozen thumbs-up poses when they walk past.
- **Crew Focus:** **Mansukh + Manya Tag Combo**. Mansukh builds vector bridges across floor hazards while Manya recolors impassable blue barrier doors.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Vanity mirror lightbulbs flickering.
  - Sign: `"WARDROBE: BUSINESS CASUAL ONLY"`
  - "Make-The-Logo-Bigger" client drones patrolling the hallways.

---

### Room 05: The Backlot Street Set
- **Scene:** Fake western storefront facades prop-up by timber scaffolding. Moving studio crane rigs swing heavy spotlights back and forth.
- **The Gag:** Sayam tries to buy the fake bank facade thinking it holds real municipal deposits.
- **Crew Focus:** **Full Crew Synergy**. Tushar freezes moving camera crane sweeps, Sehaj rewinds collapsing storefront facades, and Mansukh anchors bridges.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Wooden Western saloon facade with "BEIGE BANK" painted over it.
  - Signage: `"QUIET ON SET — AI GENERATING SCENE"`
  - Clapperboard props scattered in the dirt.

---

### Room 06: Soundstage 04 — The Seamless Horizon (Pre-Boss)
- **Scene:** A terrifyingly sterile cyclorama room with curved corners where floor, wall, and ceiling blend into seamless white-grey infinite limbo.
- **The Gag:** The crew realizes they have no shadows; Mansukh panics that the entire room has been anti-aliased into oblivion.
- **Crew Focus:** **Color Gamut Trial**. Waves of Stock Photo Interns and Memphis Blobs rush the team; Manya splashes primary colors across the room to break the void.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - CRT screen: `"Recolored enemies take bonus damage!"`
  - Floating hex code readouts: `"#FFFFFF — PURITY DETECTED"`
  - Giant studio lights humming aggressively.

---

### Room 07: The Suburban Studio Set (Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** A creepily pristine plastic suburban living room set complete with artificial astroturf and a plastic golden retriever.
- **The Gag:** The Stock Photo Family (Dad, Mom, Teenager, Dog) steps out, flashing blinding 100-watt smiles and hurling synergistic thumbs-up projectiles.
- **Crew Focus:** **Boss Encounter (The Stock Photo Family)**. Manya recolors their impenetrable beige shields, Mansukh builds ramps to avoid their lawnmower dash attacks, and Tushar stuns their group photos.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Plastic picket fence and artificial turf.
  - Family portrait sign: `"APPROVED AUTHENTIC FAMILY UNIT #812"`
  - Victory state: The set dissolves into hyper-saturated neon techno-graffiti, freeing the path toward the vertical megacity spires.
