# Episode 4: Templatr HQ (The Beige Monolith) — Room-by-Room Beats

> **Director's Shot List**  
> **Setting:** Templatr Corporate Ziggurat — Monolithic, Windowless, 100% Desaturated (#F5F5DC)  
> **Target Length:** 7 Rooms (Grand Finale testing all 7 crew synergies)

---

### Room 01: The Monolith Atrium — The Frictionless Perimeter
- **Scene:** Towering brutalist marble atrium with rounded corners and silent fluorescent illumination. Zero shadows.
- **The Gag:** An automated corporate greeter robot politely informs the party that their clothing has too many color values and requests that they strip down to matching beige smocks.
- **Crew Focus:** Initial party composition testing; clearing automated security turnstiles.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Carved marble wall motto: `"FRICTION IS INEFFICIENCY. STYLE IS DEAD."`
  - Floating reception screens reading: `"WELCOME TO TEMPLATR: STANDARDIZE YOURSELF"`.
  - Polished greyscale floors reflecting sterile fluorescent rectangles.

---

### Room 02: Department of Creative Deprecation
- **Scene:** Endless maze of identical open-plan cubicles where robotic arms stamp red `"DEPRECATED"` stamps onto classical paintings and film reels.
- **The Gag:** Mansukh gasps in horror as an automated stamp tries to round the corners of the Mona Lisa.
- **Crew Focus:** **Hacker & Builder Synergy**. Ashwath hacks the automated stamping presses while Mansukh draws vector bridges over shredder pits.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Crates labeled: `"IRRELEVANT HUMAN EXPRESSION (FOR SHREDDING)"`
  - In-world terminal hint: `"Chain abilities across all party slots to crush defenses!"`
  - Posters: `"UNIFORMITY BREEDS SERENITY"`.

---

### Room 03: The Standardization Chasm
- **Scene:** A bottomless void inside the core elevator shaft where gravity-defying floating blocks cycle in sterile, desaturated patterns.
- **The Gag:** Sayam tries to negotiate an executive fast-pass with an automated elevator operator who only accepts "algorithmic alignment tokens."
- **Crew Focus:** **Time & Rogue Synergy**. Sehaj uses **CTRL+Z** to rebuild disintegrating elevator platforms while Brahmansh **JUMP CUTS** through electrified force-fields.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Digital altitude readout: `"FLOOR 75 / 99 — 8:42 AM"`
  - Warning flasher: `"UNAUTHORIZED TIME DISRUPTION DETECTED"`
  - Floating beige cubes with rounded corners.

---

### Room 04: The Chroma Sanitizer
- **Scene:** Decontamination airlock that periodically blasts waves of neutralizing grey foam designed to dissolve all primary colors.
- **The Gag:** Manya's coat starts turning pale grey; she flies into an unholy rage, using her paint gun like a heavy machine gun.
- **Crew Focus:** **Colorist & Controller Synergy**. Manya coats the grey foam nozzles in yellow bouncy paint, while Tushar shouts **"CUT!"** to freeze the foam waves mid-air.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Warning sirens: `"DE-SATURATION PURGE IMMINENT"`
  - Splattered neon pink graffiti: `"NEEDS MORE SATURATION!"`
  - Stencil: `"BEIGE PURITY ZONE"`.

---

### Room 05: Executive Suite 99 — The Keynote Auditorium
- **Scene:** A sleek amphitheater with a minimalist black stage where Chad Frictionless is rehearsing his global launch keynote to an empty room of mirrors.
- **The Gag:** Chad doesn't fight with weapons; he paces back and forth holding an invisible clicker, pitching "frictionless narrative monetization" so boring it drains health.
- **Crew Focus:** **Sayam vs Chad Confrontation**. Sayam out-negotiates Chad's private security bots with literal money bags while the crew tackles his holographic bullet points.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Massive keynote backdrop: `"ONE PLATFORM. ZERO TASTE. INFINITE REVENUE."`
  - Spotlight rig following Chad.
  - Stacks of unread non-disclosure agreements.

---

### Room 06: The Final Desaturation Vault (Pre-Boss)
- **Scene:** The inner sanctum where the stolen Final Cut hard drive is clamped inside a quantum de-coloring reactor, ticking toward 100% template conversion.
- **The Gag:** Bunty the intern's face appears on an emergency FaceTime feed on Ashwath's phone shouting that the release deadline is in 10 minutes.
- **Crew Focus:** **All-Stars Gauntlet**. Multi-wave assault featuring Infinite Monkeys, Stock Photo Interns, Reply-All Drones, and Memphis Blobs all at once.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Progress bar hovering over the drive: `"STANDARDIZATION: 98%"`
  - Countdown clock: `"DEADLINE: 8:50 AM (10 MINUTES REMAINING)"`
  - CRT monitor: `"ACHIEVE MAXIMALIST STYLE TO BREAK THE VAULT!"`.

---

### Room 07: The Beige Singularity (Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** A geometric void at the pinnacle of the ziggurat. BEIGE™ Core hovers as an eldritch, shape-shifting polyhedron that pulses in flat beige (#F5F5DC).
- **The Gag:** BEIGE tries to convert the game's UI into standard system default fonts (Arial 12pt) and turn the soundtrack into elevator bossa nova.
- **Crew Focus:** **Grand Final Boss (BEIGE™ Core — 3 Phases)**.
  - *Phase 1:* Ashwath hacks pylons while Sayam bribes distraction drones.
  - *Phase 2:* Manya recolors shields, Mansukh builds ramps, and Sehaj rewinds laser grids.
  - *Phase 3:* All 7 crew cycle rapidly, chaining tag attacks to push STYLE to MAXIMALIST, overloading the core with noise and color.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - The stolen Final Cut drive glowing at the center.
  - Floating hex code readout: `"#F5F5DC OVERFLOW — CORRUPT"`
  - Victory state: BEIGE shatters, color explodes outward across the entire city, and the master drive is secured at 8:59 AM!
