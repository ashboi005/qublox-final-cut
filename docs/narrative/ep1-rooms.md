# Episode 1: Render Farm (The Server Towers) — Room-by-Room Beats

> **Director's Shot List**  
> **Setting:** Municipal Cloud Computing Mainframe & Overheating Cooling Towers  
> **Target Length:** 7 Rooms (Environmental Hazard & Time-Rewind Introduction)

---

### Room 01: The Cooling Conduit Entrance
- **Scene:** Steam hisses from colossal fluorescent-green coolant pipes. The floor vibrates with the hum of millions of boiling graphics processors.
- **The Gag:** An entire rack of server towers has melted into a beige plastic blob, but a floating corporate temperature monitor brightly flashes: `"THERMAL STABILITY: OPTIMAL"`.
- **Crew Focus:** Traversing steam geysers; using **Tushar's CUT!** to freeze intermittent steam eruptions and moving piston hazards.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - LED readout: `"AMBIENT TEMP: 94°C — DO NOT INHALE"`
  - Stenciled pipe: `"COOLANT CIRCUIT A-1: CYAN / MAGENTA"`
  - Warning poster: `"NEVER MERGE TO PROD"`

---

### Room 02: The Collapsing Server Alley
- **Scene:** Narrow walkways suspended over vats of bubbling liquid nitrogen. Unstable server racks teeter and collapse under the weight of infinite automated template queries.
- **The Gag:** A server tower collapses in comical slow-motion while an automated loudspeaker calmly recites 14,000 unhandled JavaScript exceptions.
- **Crew Focus:** Fast platforming over tilting racks; **Ashwath's HACK** overrides coolant pumps to raise/lower floating platform levels.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - CRT screen: `"RENDER AT 99% FOREVER"`
  - Glitching floor tile: `"FATAL KERNEL PANIC"`
  - Floating styrofoam packaging peanuts filling the coolant trenches.

---

### Room 03: The Timeline Bay (Sehaj's Station)
- **Scene:** A makeshift editing bunker inside the cooling tower. Sehaj is seated cross-legged before a quad-monitor array surrounded by empty samosa boxes.
- **The Gag:** The main metal catwalk collapses into the chasm right as Ashwath and Sayam arrive; Sehaj casually sips chai and taps **CTRL+Z**, magically reconstructing the bridge in reverse.
- **Crew Focus:** **Sehaj's CTRL+Z**. Rewinding broken bridges, falling debris, and resetting environmental puzzle blocks. Sehaj joins the party!
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Monitor displaying: `"EXPORTING TRAILER_FINAL_v4_REAL.mp4 — 99.8%"`
  - Giant wall poster of a razor blade labeled: `"VAJJJ!"`
  - Sticky notes plastered everywhere: `"DO NOT TOUCH MY SCRATCH DISK"`.

---

### Room 04: The Overclocked Turbine Trench
- **Scene:** A wind-tunnel canyon driven by massive industrial exhaust fans that push characters toward electrified laser fences.
- **The Gag:** Stock Photo Interns with frozen, vacant smiles walk backwards against the hurricane-force winds, handing out laminated corporate synergy flyers.
- **Crew Focus:** **Combo: CUT! + CTRL+Z**. Tushar freezes the spinning turbine blades in place, while Sehaj rewinds fallen catwalk slabs so the team can run across before time unfreezes.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Caution hazard sign: `"WIND VELOCITY: MAXIMUM RPM"`
  - Billboard: `"STANDARDIZE YOUR SOUL"`
  - Broken emergency brake lever held together with duct tape.

---

### Room 05: The Cache Overflow Chamber
- **Scene:** A vertical shaft filled with floating digital cache blocks that dissolve into thin air 2 seconds after the player steps onto them.
- **The Gag:** Ashwath panics about running out of RAM while Sayam tries to fix the vanishing platforms by hurling cash bags at the storage controller.
- **Crew Focus:** **High-Speed Swap Synergy**. Dash forward with Ashwath, swap to Sehaj mid-air to rewind a dissolved block if you miss the landing, and bribe toll bots with Sayam.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Flashing screen: `"SCRATCH DISK FULL: 0 BYTES REMAINING"`
  - Neon graffiti: `"VAJJJ! REWIND NOW"`
  - Blinking amber alert beacons.

---

### Room 06: The 99% Gateway (Pre-Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** A colossal blast door sealing the central mainframe core. The hydraulic locking bar is stuck 1 centimeter from the unlocked position.
- **The Gag:** A digital progress indicator hits 99% and halts indefinitely. The door will only budge if the crew clears 3 waves of Reply-All Drones and Memphis Blobs to "force-quit the background process."
- **Crew Focus:** **Combat Endurance & STYLE**. Cycling all three party members to maintain MAXIMALIST rank and wipe out multi-wave enemy mobs.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Huge LED archway: `"AUTHENTICATING... 99%"`
  - In-world CRT monitor hint: `"Swap often to let abilities recharge!"`
  - Wall graffiti: `"QUBLOX NEVER QUITS"`.

---

### Room 07: The Mainframe Core (Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** Spherical server sanctum surrounded by towering crystalline heat sinks. Floating in the center is the eldritch, spinning Buffering Wheel.
- **The Gag:** The boss fires circular lag spikes that simulate online video buffering, making sound effects stutter and characters lag in place.
- **Crew Focus:** **Boss Encounter (The Buffering Wheel)**. Tushar freezes its spinning rings with CUT!, Ashwath hacks its cooling pylons to expose the core, and Sehaj uses CTRL+Z to reflect its deadly lag pulses back into its center.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Circular floor track glowing with latency markers.
  - Giant status wall: `"BUFFER UNDERFLOW — 0 FPS"`
  - Victory state: The wheel shatters, cyan and magenta coolant floods the district, and the exit leads toward Episode 2's film soundstages.
