# Episode 0: Cold Open (Qublox HQ) — Room-by-Room Beats

> **Director's Shot List**  
> **Setting:** Qublox Studio Penthouse & HQ Infrastructure  
> **Target Length:** 7 Rooms (Tutorial & Mechanics Introduction)

---

### Room 01: The Penthouse Studio
- **Scene:** 11:58 PM. Neon monitors flicker against exposed concrete. The Final Cut drive is abducted through the AC vent by a mechanical BEIGE™ claw.
- **The Gag:** Ashwath types furiously with both hands and one foot while Sayam relaxes on a velvet beanbag sipping elaichi chai.
- **Crew Focus:** Basic movement, jump, Ashwath basic attack (keyboard "CLACK"), and Sayam ranged cash toss ("LE RE!").
- **Props & Signage:** 
  - CRT screen: `"DELIVERY: 9:00 AM — EXPORT HANGING"`
  - Whiteboard filled with panicky scribbles: `"HOTFIX #992 — DO NOT TOUCH"`
  - Half-empty chai glasses and mechanical keyboards scattered everywhere.

---

### Room 02: The Bleaching Corridor
- **Scene:** The main office corridor where the walls are visibly losing color, fading from vibrant graffiti into dull institutional beige.
- **The Gag:** A Corporate Memphis Blob is diligently repainting retro-wave street art with beige latex paint using a comically tiny roller.
- **Crew Focus:** **Ashwath's HACK**. A security barrier and ceiling camera block the path; hacking the terminal opens the shutter and turns the camera away.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - In-world CRT monitor showing hint: `"Press 1/2/3 to swap crew members instantly!"`
  - Neon poster on wall: `"MAXIMALIST OR DIE"`
  - Flickering fluorescent tube overhead buzzing in B-flat.

---

### Room 03: The Executive Turnstile
- **Scene:** An automated high-security glass turnstile installed by corporate landlord bots that demands an exorbitant microtransaction toll.
- **The Gag:** An automated security guard drone refuses passage on ethical grounds until Sayam tosses a thick bag of rupee bills at its face.
- **Crew Focus:** **Sayam's BRIBE**. Throwing money at the toll receptor unlocks the gate and distracts the patrolling drones.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Digital banner: `"TOLL: 50,000 CR — NO CHECKS"`
  - Graffiti on turnstile: `"SAYAM PAYS CASH"`
  - A vending machine selling stale potato chips for 2,000 credits.

---

### Room 04: The 3rd-Floor Maglev Balcony
- **Scene:** Open-air exterior balcony overlooking Neo-Amritsar's neon skyline. A high-voltage gap spans between two studio wings.
- **The Gag:** Chacha Chaiwala is nonchalantly brewing ginger tea on a floating maglev cart over a 200-meter vertical drop; Pixel the cat watches from an edge.
- **Crew Focus:** **Tag-team swap momentum**. Leaping over the wide pit as Sayam and swapping to Ashwath mid-jump preserves forward velocity to reach the ledge.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Chacha Chaiwala's cart with smoking brass kettle.
  - Signboard: `"CUTTING CHAI 50 CR — NO BEIGE DRINKS"`
  - Broken fiber-optic cables dangling and sparking into the abyss.

---

### Room 05: The Basement Gear Cage
- **Scene:** Concrete equipment lockup crammed with vintage cinema lenses, anamorphic rigs, and heavy camera cranes.
- **The Gag:** A troop of Infinite Monkeys has trapped Tushar atop a lighting truss; they are furiously banging on Remington typewriters, throwing crumpled drafts at him.
- **Crew Focus:** **Tushar's CUT!**. Freezing the monkey projectiles and typing frenzy in place for 3 seconds allows the crew to knock them out. Tushar joins party!
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Wooden clapperboards and rolled cinema backdrops.
  - Stenciled floor warning: `"BAN ALL MONKEYS — CINEMA HAZARD"`
  - Mountains of discarded typewriter ribbon.

---

### Room 06: The Ventilation Intake Hub
- **Scene:** Vertical maintenance shaft with rotating industrial fan blades and laser security grids leading to the main server hub.
- **The Gag:** Bunty the Intern is wedged in an intake grate, gripping an emergency magnetic tape drive between his teeth to keep it from falling.
- **Crew Focus:** **Full 3-Member Tag Synergy**. Ashwath hacks the laser grid, Tushar freezes the spinning fan blades with CUT!, and Sayam bribes the ventilation toll drone.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - CRT screen: `"Chain swap attacks to pump STYLE to MAXIMALIST!"`
  - Wall stencil: `"DO NOT HIT REPLY ALL"`
  - Warning flasher: `"AIR INTAKE 100% BEIGE CONTAMINATED"`

---

### Room 07: The Grand Server Lobby (Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** Cavernous octagonal server hall. In the center, the ceiling opens and the Reply-All Mothership descends in a flurry of notification halos.
- **The Gag:** The boss fires calendar invite pop-ups, CC's everyone in the room, and deafens the party with synthetic "ding!" alert chimes.
- **Crew Focus:** **Boss Encounter**. Swap combos: hack shields with Ashwath, freeze missile barrages with Tushar, and use Sayam's cash to redirect drone swarms.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Massive CRT scoreboard: `"THREAD STATUS: UNRESOLVED (4,812 REPLIES)"`
  - Shattered server glass displaying matrix rain that has turned beige.
  - Exit freight elevator marked: `"TRANSIT TICKET TO RENDER FARM"`.
