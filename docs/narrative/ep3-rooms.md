# Episode 3: The Feed (Infinite-Scroll Megatower) — Room-by-Room Beats

> **Director's Shot List**  
> **Setting:** Vertical Attention-Economy Spire with Constant Upward Infinite Scroll  
> **Target Length:** 7 Rooms (Jump Cut Teleportation & High-Speed Vertical Platforming)

---

### Room 01: The Scroll Base — Ground Level Dopamine Intake
- **Scene:** The ground lobby of the Feed Spire. Huge neon fiber columns pulse with trending hashtags. The ceiling is open all the way to the stratosphere.
- **The Gag:** Infinite Monkeys sit at high-speed mechanical desks hitting "Repost" buttons on conveyor belts.
- **Crew Focus:** Vertical climbing basics with Ashwath, Sehaj, and Tushar.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Giant LED ticker: `"TRENDING: BEIGE IS THE NEW NEON (PROMOTED)"`
  - Floor sign: `"INFINITE SCROLL ENGAGED — KEEP SCROLLING"`
  - Piles of dopamine notification pills.

---

### Room 02: The Trending Elevator — Continuous Vertical Scroll
- **Scene:** An open-air freight platform ascending rapidly as the lower floor disintegrates into a void of deleted posts.
- **The Gag:** The floor is actively scrolling upward; if characters stand still for more than 2 seconds, the screen pushes them off the bottom into algorithmic obscurity.
- **Crew Focus:** Vertical reflex platforming; dodging falling popup ads and clickbait banners.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Floating signs: `"YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT HAPPENS NEXT"`
  - Red notification bubbles with numbers in the millions.
  - Caution hazard: `"FALL HAZARD: ALGORITHMIC PURGE"`.

---

### Room 03: The Paywall Firewall — Brahmansh's Heist
- **Scene:** A corridor barred by solid laser paywalls with zero keyholes, doors, or hacking terminals.
- **The Gag:** Brahmansh is sitting on the other side of the solid barrier, casually drinking iced chai, having bypassed security via jump cut.
- **Crew Focus:** **Brahmansh's JUMP CUT**. Instant 4-tile teleport that passes directly through thin walls, laser barriers, and enemy mobs. Brahmansh joins party!
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Glowing red barrier: `"PAYWALL: SUBSCRIBE FOR 99.99/MO TO PASS"`
  - Stencil: `"P-A-C-I-N-G IS EVERYTHING"`
  - Broken timeline film strips on floor.

### Room 04: The Echo Chamber
- **Scene:** Cylindrical chamber where sound bounces in deafening resonant loops, creating sonic shockwaves that push players around.
- **The Gag:** Reply-All Drones flying in circles shouting "I AGREE WITH MY COLLEAGUE!" into megaphones.
- **Crew Focus:** **Brahmansh Rogue Combat**. Double jump and Jump Cut teleport through the sonic wave rings to assassinate the drone megaphones from behind.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Neon acoustic panels flashing: `"CONSENSUS DETECTED"`
  - Signage: `"NO DISSENTING OPINIONS PERMITTED"`
  - Echoing synthetic laughter tracks.

---

### Room 05: The Autoplay Spiral
- **Scene:** A spiraling helix catwalk wrapping around the exterior of the tower in the open rain. Autoplay video ads pop up in front of the camera to blind the player.
- **The Gag:** Fake "X" close buttons on popup ads that actually spawn more enemies when clicked.
- **Crew Focus:** **Speed Synergy**. Brahmansh teleports past the popup hitboxes, Mansukh builds ramps over broken helix sections, and Sayam bribes toll gates.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Floating popup ad: `"[X] CLOSE (SPAWNS 4 MONKEYS)"`
  - Neon sign: `"SKIP AD IN 5... 4... 3... (NEVER)"`
  - Neon city lights glittering below through the digital smog.

---

### Room 06: The Rage-Bait Refinery (Pre-Boss)
- **Scene:** Boiling data refineries generating fiery red rage-reaction icons that rain from the ceiling like mortar fire.
- **The Gag:** The enemies don't have weapons; they just scream inflammatory headline hot-takes that deal emotional psychic damage.
- **Crew Focus:** **Mob Clearing at S-Rank**. Chaining Brahmansh's lightning-fast timeline cuts with Tushar's freeze and Sehaj's razor slashes.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - CRT screen: `"Chain jump cuts to maintain MAXIMALIST STYLE!"`
  - Fireballs shaped like angry emojis.
  - Signboard: `"OUTRAGE GENERATION AT 100% EFFICIENCY"`.

---

### Room 07: The Algorithmic Nexus (Boss Arena)
- **Scene:** The summit pinnacle of the Feed Tower. The floor is a glass infinity mirror looking down through 200 floors of digital architecture.
- **The Gag:** The Algorithm manifests as a shape-shifting chimera of clickbait thumbnails, spinning autoplay wheels, and rage-bait headlines that constantly morphs its attack patterns.
- **Crew Focus:** **Boss Encounter (The Algorithm)**. Brahmansh jump-cuts through its invulnerable forward shields to strike its unbuffered timeline core from behind, while Sehaj rewinds its rage-bait mortar volleys.
- **Props & Signage:**
  - Giant holographic headline banner changing every 5 seconds.
  - Metric counters: `"ENGAGEMENT: CRITICAL OVERLOAD"`
  - Victory state: The Algorithm de-indexes itself, the vertical spire stabilizes, and the transit beam opens directly to Templatr HQ.
