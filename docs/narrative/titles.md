# Title Pitches: QUBLOX

Ten proposed titles for the game, pitched by Gemini. The current working title is **QUBLOX: FINAL CUT**.

---

### Top 3 Recommendations

1. **QUBLOX: FINAL CUT** ★ *(Top Pick / Working Title)*  
   *Why:* Instantly communicates cinema/post-production stakes, sounds like an auteur director's definitive edition, and anchors the literal McGuffin master drive.

2. **QUBLOX: OVERFLOW** ★ *(Top Pick)*  
   *Why:* Double-entendre capturing both Ashwath's stack-overflow developer panic and the visual sensory explosion of maximalist color bursting through beige corporate walls.

3. **NEO-AMRITSAR: CTRL+Z** ★ *(Top Pick)*  
   *Why:* Plants the unique Punjabi retro-futurist setting front-and-center alongside Sehaj's reality-rewinding time mechanic.

---

### Additional Options

4. **DEATH BY TEMPLATE**  
   *Why:* Dark, punchy corporate satire that immediately frames the hilarious horror of an AI-generated desaturated apocalypse.

5. **QUBLOX: 99% RENDERED**  
   *Why:* Visceral gut-punch for every video editor and animator who has watched an export bar freeze at the dawn of a deadline.

6. **BLEACHED: A QUBLOX ODYSSEY**  
   *Why:* Emphasizes the visual stakes of the world losing its color palette while maintaining classic arcade adventure swagger.

7. **QUBLOX: MAXIMALIST**  
   *Why:* Direct, elegant statement of identity honoring the top S-rank STYLE meter and the crew's creative philosophy.

8. **MERGE CONFLICT: 2084**  
   *Why:* Merges developer trauma with retro-futuristic sci-fi worldbuilding in a snappy two-word hook.

9. **QUBLOX: LE RE!**  
   *Why:* Bold, hilarious inside-baseball nod to Sayam's cash-tossing catchphrase that sets an irreverent street-level comedic tone.

10. **RGB MUST DIE (OR WE DO)**  
    *Why:* High-energy 80s action movie subtitle parody highlighting the war between vivid primary colors and corporate greyscale.
